About Marietta Dental Professionals

A welcoming dental home where modern dentistry, clear communication, and personalized care come together for Marietta and East Cobb families.

Who we are

Built around the way patients deserve to be treated.

Marietta Dental Professionals is a family and cosmetic dental practice serving patients throughout Marietta and East Cobb, Georgia. Our approach is simple: listen carefully, explain clearly, and provide thoughtful dental care that fits the person sitting in the chair.

Our practice brings preventive, restorative, cosmetic, pediatric, emergency, and implant dentistry together in one convenient location. Whether you are due for a routine checkup, interested in improving your smile, or need help with an urgent dental concern, our team is here to guide you through your options.

Modern tools. Human communication.

Technology is an important part of contemporary dentistry, but it should never replace a personal conversation. We use modern dental tools to support accurate diagnosis, efficient treatment planning, and clearer patient education. At the same time, our team takes time to answer questions and explain recommendations in straightforward language.

We also understand that dental visits can feel uncomfortable or unfamiliar. Creating a calm, welcoming experience is part of our everyday approach. Our goal is to help you feel heard, informed, and comfortable from the moment you arrive.

Prevention is part of the plan.

Healthy smiles are supported by more than treating problems after they appear. We encourage regular examinations, professional cleanings, good home-care habits, and timely attention to changes in your mouth. By focusing on prevention and education, we help patients take a more active role in their long-term oral health.

Our commitment to patients

Every patient brings different priorities, questions, and circumstances to the dental office. That is why we avoid a one-size-fits-all approach. We work to make recommendations that reflect your oral health, goals, comfort, and treatment needs.

Our vision is to be a trusted dental home for the Marietta and East Cobb community — a place where patients can return for dependable care throughout every stage of life.
